# Privacy Policy for DVIDE

**Effective Date:** August 13, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 13, 2026

## 1. Overview
DVIDE ("the Application") is a salary-cycle personal finance tracker for Android developed by Surajit Das ("we", "us", or "our"). We understand the sensitive nature of personal financial data and are committed to complete privacy and data isolation.

## 2. Financial Data Storage & Isolation
- **100% Offline & On-Device Storage:** All income entries, expenditure logs, custom categories, budget allocations, and settings are stored **exclusively on your device** using local SQLite/Room databases and DataStore preferences.
- **No Cloud Uploads or Server Connectivity:** We do not host, store, or transmit your financial information to any external server.
- **No Bank Account Credentials:** DVIDE is a manual tracking tool. It never asks for, connects to, or stores bank credentials, credit card details, or financial account Logins.

## 3. Device Permissions
- The Application requires **no special Android runtime permissions** for its core features.
- No network permission (`INTERNET`) is requested, ensuring network-level isolation of your data.

## 4. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Ads
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** We do not collect telemetries, usage stats, or error reports using third-party SDKs.
- **No Advertisements:** DVIDE contains no third-party advertisements or ad networks.

## 5. Security & Data Retention
Your financial records remain strictly within your device's app sandbox. Uninstalling DVIDE will permanently erase all locally stored transactions and settings unless you make a local manual backup.

## 6. Children's Privacy
DVIDE does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be published in this document within the project repository.
